Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how KidneyTrack handles information when you use the KidneyTrack mobile application. KidneyTrack is a personal kidney care tracking app for recording information such as fluid intake and output, medicines, health profile details, dialysis schedule, dialysis center details, and dialysis session weights.

KidneyTrack is intended as a personal tracking tool. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or professional medical advice.

Information You Enter

KidneyTrack may store information that you choose to enter into the app, including:

  • Health profile details, such as CKD stage, dialysis status, diabetes status, elevated potassium or phosphorus flags, and fluid restriction setting.
  • Fluid intake and output entries.
  • Medicine names, dosage details, schedules, stock information, and dose logs.
  • Dialysis schedule details, clinic name, clinic address, and dialysis session pre-weight and post-weight.
  • App settings, such as notification preferences and daily fluid limit.

How Information Is Stored

The information you enter is stored locally on your device. KidneyTrack does not require an account and does not upload your personal health data to a server operated by us.

Information Sharing

KidneyTrack does not sell your information. KidneyTrack does not share the personal health information you enter with advertisers, analytics providers, or other third parties.

Notifications

If you enable medicine reminders, KidneyTrack uses your device's local notification system to remind you about scheduled medicines. Notification data is processed on your device for app functionality.

Dialysis Center Data

KidneyTrack includes a bundled list of dialysis centers as reference information. This list is stored inside the app and can be searched or viewed without sending your search activity to us.

Permissions

KidneyTrack may request notification permission so it can send local reminders. The app does not require location, camera, microphone, contacts, or account permissions for its core tracking features.

Data Retention and Deletion

Information you enter remains on your device unless you delete it in the app, clear the app's data through your device settings, or uninstall the app. Because KidneyTrack does not maintain an online account for you, we cannot remotely access, recover, or delete data stored only on your device.

Security

KidneyTrack stores app data locally using the device's app storage. You are responsible for keeping your device secure, including using screen lock, operating system updates, and device backup settings appropriate for sensitive health information.

Children's Privacy

KidneyTrack is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
